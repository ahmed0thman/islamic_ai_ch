## Quran text and typeface

- **Quran text:** the King Fahd Complex file `tools/data/qurancomplex/hafsData_v2-0.json`
  (Hafs, Madinah Mushaf). Rights belong to the King Fahd Complex for the Printing of
  the Holy Quran. The terms of use have **not yet been reviewed**: the Complex's
  website did not respond, and the check is pending on a local machine. It is used
  here for display and matching, with attribution to the Complex.
- **Typeface:** `app/src/fonts/uthmanic_hafs_v20.ttf` (the Complex's font, v20). Rights
  belong to the King Fahd Complex. Its terms have **not yet been reviewed**, and no
  licence text for it was found in the repository. It is used here for displaying the
  verses, with attribution.
- **`tools/data/quran-simple-clean.json`:** a downloaded Quran text file kept only for
  comparison. The licences register does not record its terms; they have **not been
  reviewed**.

## Quotations from books

Short quotations from books of tafsir and related works appear in `content/`. Each is
attributed by book and locator; the rights in those books belong to their authors and
publishers. Full book texts are not published in this repository. The books and the
terms of the platforms they were taken from are listed in the register above, and
most of those platform terms are **not yet reviewed**.

The application's packages and fonts keep their own licences (mostly MIT and
